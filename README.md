# Leonardo Mesquita Pinto

PhD in Tropical Marine Sciences (Federal University of Ceará, 2023), with a
background in Environmental Sciences.

I am an aquatic ecologist who works with data. My research describes how
environmental gradients and human pressures shape fish assemblages in
estuaries, rivers and coastal systems, and how that information can support
conservation and management decisions. Most of it ends up as code: curating
field and monitoring databases, estimating diversity and distribution metrics,
and building reproducible pipelines in R and Python.

## What I work on

* **Fish assemblage ecology.** Spatial and temporal patterns of taxonomic and
  functional diversity, using trait based approaches, Hill numbers and kernel
  density hypervolumes.
* **Conservation assessment.** IUCN range metrics and threat assessment for
  freshwater fishes, including a state level red list.
* **Distribution modelling and global change.** Correlative models combined
  with land use and climate scenarios to quantify anthropic pressure on
  suitable habitat.
* **Ecosystem modelling and fisheries.** Mass balance models (Ecopath with
  Ecosim) and fishing effort simulations, plus characterization of artisanal
  fisheries and associated local knowledge.
* **Biodiversity data curation.** Standardization and quality control of
  multi source, multi year datasets, taxonomic harmonization and spatial
  consistency checks.

## Selected repositories

* **[climate-landuse-risk-antenori](https://github.com/leopinto718/climate-landuse-risk-antenori):**
  species distribution modelling combined with MapBiomas land use data to
  quantify anthropic pressure on the suitable habitat of an endemic Caatinga
  killifish. 43.8% of the climatically suitable area is already under
  anthropic use.
* **[automated_range_metrics_freshwater_fish](https://github.com/leopinto718/automated_range_metrics_freshwater_fish):**
  automated pipeline for IUCN range metrics (EOO and AOO) based on hydrographic
  sub basins, supporting a published regional conservation assessment
  ([doi](https://doi.org/10.1590/1982-0224-2024-0084)).
* **[remove-outliers-lw-app](https://github.com/leopinto718/remove-outliers-lw-app):**
  Shiny application for detecting and removing outliers in length weight data,
  a routine quality control step in fish biometry.


## Tools

R as my main language, Python for automation and scripting. Spatial analysis
with sf, terra and ArcGIS. Shiny for small interactive tools. Multivariate and
functional ecology, generalized linear models, diversity estimation.

## Beyond code

Peer reviewed articles in international journals, two illustrated fish
identification guides (estuarine fishes of the Brazilian semiarid coast, 2023;
continental fishes of Ceará, 2026) and book chapters on artisanal fishing
communities. I also taught five undergraduate courses as a substitute lecturer
at UFC, including an introduction to R for students with no prior programming
experience.
