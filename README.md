## Leonardo Mesquita Pinto

PhD in Tropical Marine Sciences (UFC), currently taking an MBA in Data Science
(UNIFOR) and a degree in Systems Analysis and Development (UniFanor).

I work at the interface of quantitative ecology and data science: modelling
human pressures on biodiversity and ecosystem services, combining land-use data,
climate scenarios and spatial indicators to support conservation decisions.
I build reproducible pipelines in R and Python.

**Selected projects**

- **climate-landuse-risk-antenori** — species distribution modelling combined
  with MapBiomas land-use data to quantify anthropic pressure on the suitable
  habitat of an endemic Caatinga killifish. 43.8% of the climatically suitable
  area is already under anthropic use.
- **automated_range_metrics_freshwater_fish** — automated pipeline for IUCN
  range metrics (EOO/AOO) based on hydrographic sub-basins, supporting a
  published regional conservation assessment (https://doi.org/10.1590/1982-0224-2024-0084).
- **valoracao_manguezais_municipios_brasileiros** — economic valuation of
  carbon stored by mangroves across Brazilian municipalities.

**Methods** — species distribution modelling, ecosystem modelling (Ecopath with
Ecosim), multivariate and functional ecology, spatial analysis and GIS.
